# Incident Response and Suspension

## Safe halt

Critical defects or credential compromise may trigger scoped safe halt via `OperationalSuspension`.

## Suspension effects

May disable: new access, affected workflows, credentials, integrations, AI tools, public availability.

Must preserve: records, audit, evidence, appeal/redress access where possible.

## AI incidents

On AI use case suspension, revoke agent credentials. Core human workflows continue where authorized.
