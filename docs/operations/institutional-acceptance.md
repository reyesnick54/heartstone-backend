# Institutional Acceptance

Institutional acceptance is distinct from technical readiness, CI success, pilot success, and vendor attestation.

## Progression

1. Technical readiness (engineering verification)
2. Institutional acceptance (authorized officeholder decision)
3. Operational activation (launch gate + accountable owner)

## Rules

- Expired acceptance cannot remain valid indefinitely.
- Residual risks must be explicitly accepted by proper authority owners.
- Critical defects and known limitations must appear in acceptance evidence.
